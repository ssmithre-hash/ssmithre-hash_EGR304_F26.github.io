## Introduction

This datasheet documents Stellan Smith-Rel's individual contributions to the EGR 304 Team 203 project. It provides an overview of the project, outlines my responsibilities, and links to the supporting engineering documentation.

### Project Summary

Our team is developing a customizable cat toy that integrates multiple interactive components, including a motor, speaker, microphone, and pressure sensor. These components work together to provide a flexible and engaging play experience for cats.

For additional details about the project, please refer to our [team report](https://egr304-2026-f-203.github.io/EGR304_Team203.github.io/).

### My Contribution

My primary contribution is the design and development of the system's central control board, which serves as the primary interface for communication between the toy's various subsystems. This board is responsible for receiving and transmitting signals to other boards, as well as controlling the speaker component.
