## Overview

This schematic is designed to support the hub-spoke operation of a multi-part cat toy, with this being the hub. There are multiple connectors to allow for off-board communication. Additionally, there is a simple speaker circuit that does not require a H-Bridge to operate, instead using an AB Amp, and a voltage regulator to supply 5V power to all circuit nets. The Curiosity Nano microcontroller manages all aspects of the circuit.
